Cantos

A creature's "story" (as outlined on a creature's sheet) is used to summarize chapters of useful and pivotal experiences into cantos.

Each canto signifies a great deal of time, effort, and survival. Select 4 skills to gain or specialize in as a result of these experiences. (select skills that loosely represent your feats and experiences.)

Backgrounds

A new player-character must select a background for their story to start with. Select one from the list of backgrounds at the end of this section, and write it in your "Story" section like so:

Canto 0 : <Type> <Short summary of character background.>

Then, be sure to list the skills gained by your background in your "Skills" section. Your character's future experiences will be noted Canto 1, 2, 3 ... Work with the GM to customize your progress in interesting ways.

Skills

These skills are mainly used in downtime actions and roleplay. In New World, downtime actions and skill checks work through these freeform narrative skills instead of stats:

  • Narrative Play: Describe what you do. If the outcome is uncertain or risky, the GM calls for a Skill Check (1d6 + relevant Skill bonus).

  • Downtime Actions: Between active tasks, players get Downtime Actions. Most downtime actions will call for a roll, most of which have an associated skill or skills to assist said action.

Possessions

  • Creatures naturally have 5 slots of inventory.
  • A standard Alteri bundle has 10 slots of inventory.
  • Items can take up one or many slots.
  • Some clothing and accessories can add slots, and some larger items may need dedicated equipment to carry.
